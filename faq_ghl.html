That's exactly how I'd encourage you to evaluate us.

The when/then trap

There's a mindset trap I see constantly. It sounds completely logical.

  • When work slows down, then I'll start.
  • When I have more money, then I'll invest in myself.
  • When life gets less busy, then I'll finally go after what I want.

The problem is that day almost never arrives. Life doesn't suddenly become less busy. You create space by making different decisions.

Nobody says when I'm fit, then I'll start going to the gym. Or when I know how to swim, then I'll get in the pool.

You become fit by going to the gym. You learn to swim by getting in the water. The action comes first, and the confidence follows.

The people who have the least time are usually the people who benefit most from building a skill that gives some of that time back.

That's exactly how I escaped Walmart. Not because I woke up with more free time. Because I decided to use the little free time I already had differently.
